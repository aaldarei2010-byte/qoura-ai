Care guides

Common reasons plants get sad.

💧

Overwatering

Yellow droopy leaves, mushy stems, soil that won't dry. The #1 killer of houseplants.

☀️

Light burn

Crispy brown patches, faded color. Move it back from the window, or filter the sun.

🐛

Pests

Sticky residue, webbing, tiny bugs on the undersides. Isolate, wipe, treat.
