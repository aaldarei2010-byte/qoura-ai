(02) — Our Story

Turning home skills into livelihoods.

🤝

We don't just sell food — we sell the story of the productive family.

Top Ten began because so many families in Al Qua'a had real skill in Emirati food, sweets and drinks — but no fixed outlet, little marketing, and no organized space to grow.

So we built a local platform that gathers their products in one place, reaches customers every day, and keeps the Emirati flavor and hospitality at its heart.

Local. Emirati. Made by our own families in Al Qua'a.
