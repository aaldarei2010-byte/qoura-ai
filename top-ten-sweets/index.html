Our Story

More than sweets — a warm welcome on every plate.

Top Ten Sweets began with a simple belief: that the best memories are made around something sweet. From bubbling pots of golden syrup to trays of pistachio-dusted treats, every recipe is crafted by hand with the same care we'd give our own family.

Whether you're grabbing a box of mithai for a celebration, sharing kunafa with friends, or simply pausing for a quiet cup of karak, our doors — and our hearts — are open.
